Why your bank’s own number can appear on a scam call

Caller ID is not proof of anything

The number your phone displays is supplied by whoever placed the call, and it can be set to anything. Seeing your bank’s real number does not mean your bank is calling.

There is a useful tell. Numbers starting 13, 1300 and 1800 are inbound service lines — under the Australian numbering plan they cannot make outgoing calls at all. So if one of those shows as your caller, the caller ID has definitely been forged. Some organisations go further and publish numbers they will never ring you from; those are marked never calls you above.

The reverse is also true and worth remembering: those same numbers are perfectly safe for you to ring. The direction is what matters.
